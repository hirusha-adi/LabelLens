An empty status includes unconfirmed permission and must not be interpreted as a sourced national ban. A positive flag applies only to the exact substance, preparation, purpose and conditions established by the cited law. Inventory presence alone was not treated as US food approval. Country bans are listed only when an explicit prohibition or withdrawal was established.

The check icon means no ban or notable regulator concern was found in the retained sources; it does not establish absence of risk. Alert icons cover recorded prohibitions, exposure exceedances, unresolved safety assessments and specific labelling or handling concerns. The feed-handling alerts describe occupational or feed findings separately from effects of eating food.

Plant or animal identity does not establish all processing aids, vegetarian suitability or halal/kosher certification. Numerical limits retain their chemical basis and group scope. Veterinary residue ADIs are not presented as ADIs authorising direct addition to food.
