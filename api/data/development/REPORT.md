The name and retained category fields come from the supplied master. The inputs property in provenance records makes that local source explicit. Some general descriptions restate that supplied classification and use the EU functional definitions; their inputs property also records this. Compound manufacturing, safety, intake and legal statements have separate external sources.

Permission could not be established for every legacy or broad identity. An empty flag records an unconfirmed permission as well as absence of a confirmed permission; it must not be interpreted as a sourced country ban. Positive flags are limited to the permitted compound, purpose and conditions. Direct US food-use permissions include affirmative GRAS regulations; food-contact-only references and veterinary-feed-only uses were excluded. An FDA inventory entry by itself was not treated as approval.

The check icon means no ban or notable concern was identified in the retained sources. It does not establish absence of risk. Alert icons cover recorded prohibitions, regulator-identified exposure exceedances, unresolved assessment data, specific labelling precautions and other retained regulatory concerns.

Plant or animal origin does not establish all processing aids or halal/kosher certification. These limits are recorded in the dietary text and gaps. Numerical limits retain their chemical basis and relevant group scope. Veterinary residue ADIs were not represented as food-additive ADIs.
